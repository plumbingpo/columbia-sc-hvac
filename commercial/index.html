Kitchen Climate Control

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and an undersized or failing system reveals itself as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts and the stuffy dining room right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Fast Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Each hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Columbia HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Columbia and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and document the entire call for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

A scheduled maintenance program from Columbia HVAC is built around your property's specific equipment, how the space is used, and your budget. Visits include inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes at the right MERV rating, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep before peak demand. Nothing controls commercial HVAC costs better than preventing failures in the first place.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Clients who switch to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
